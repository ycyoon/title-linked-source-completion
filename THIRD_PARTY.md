# Third-party attribution

`code/musique_metrics.py` extracts the official MuSiQue `normalize_answer`, `get_tokens`, `compute_exact` and `compute_f1` functions from the pinned evaluator. The source hash and extraction are recorded in `protocols/code_provenance.json`. Original project: https://github.com/StonyBrookNLP/musique . The answer normalization and token F1 in `code/metrics.py` follow the HotpotQA and 2Wiki evaluators, including the HotpotQA yes/no/noanswer convention. Original evaluators: https://github.com/hotpotqa/hotpot/blob/master/hotpot_evaluate_v1.py and https://github.com/Alab-NII/2wikimultihop . Their authors retain their original rights.

The LLMLingua implementation and all model weights are separately obtained from their original providers and are not included here. The native-cap calibration wrapper is research code for the configuration described in the manuscript. Original benchmark and Wikipedia text is also separately obtained; this artifact supplies identifiers and hashes, not those corpora.

MuSiQue evaluator source: https://github.com/StonyBrookNLP/musique/blob/922ac98f19a201998dbdae6d7f2887a5258dbdeb/metrics/answer.py .
